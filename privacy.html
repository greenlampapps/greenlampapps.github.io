Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy outlines how information is handled when you visit our website or use our mobile applications.

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Overview

GreenLampApps creates simple, practical utilities and applications (such as the Age Calculator) designed for everyday use. We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping data collection minimal and transparent.

2. Information We Collect

Website: Our website acts as a portfolio and informational page. We do not track or store personal user accounts. Standard hosting server logs may temporarily note technical details (like IP address or browser type) for security and performance purposes.

Mobile Applications: Most of our apps operate independently on your device. Any calculations or data entered into tools like the Age Calculator are processed locally on your device and are never sent to or stored on external servers.

3. Third-Party Downloads (Uptodown)

Our website provides links directing you to official platforms like Uptodown to download our applications. Once you leave our site for a third-party platform, their respective privacy policies and terms of service apply to your download and interaction.

4. Children's Privacy

Our applications and website do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Our utilities are safe, general-purpose tools suitable for all audiences.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if we introduce new apps or website updates. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with a revised date.
